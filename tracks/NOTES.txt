Attention! All these tracks require:
* Proper enduro bike, 150kg or less (anything heavier at your own risk).
* 100% enduro tyres (not the stock 50/50!), better if with rim locks (to lower pressure if you get stuck).
* Full protection: tall enduro boots, knees, elbows, gloves, and helmet. Roost guard highly recommended.
* Supplies: full tank of gas, 4 liters of water minimum, snacks.

Please don’t ride alone!

Difficulty scale:
1of5 - easy, almost boring.
2of5 - easy-ish, more technical, but still beginner-friendly.
3of5 - intermediate, you need significant technical riding experience, don’t ride this alone.
4of5 - advanced.
5of5 - advanced masochists.

Wet conditions: rule of thumb is to always add 1 level of difficulty when terrain is wet. Read the descriptions.


----------------------------------------------------
01-1of5-Mae-Rim-North.gpx
----------------------------------------------------
Difficulty: 1/5.
Notes: Easy track passing through farms and nice rice paddies.

Terrain: 20% paved (concrete), 60% double (easy dirt), 20% single (easy gravel).
Length: 70km (6 hours).
Wet conditions: might get slippery on some dirt sections, but no major difficulties.
Up/downhills: mostly flat (only 1 short hilly section).
Shaded: 30% shaded.


----------------------------------------------------
02-2of5-Ban-Thi-Doi-Ngum-Lamphun.gpx
----------------------------------------------------
Difficulty: 2/5.
Notes: Single and double track are flowy and fun. At km 18 and km 25, there are 2 nice reservoirs for breaks. Towards the end, there is a long-ish paved twisty section, going up a mountain with a viewpoint.

Length: 50km (4 hours).
Terrain: 40% paved (twisty mountain), 30% double (easy, dirt), 30% single (easy-medium dirt, gravel).
Wet conditions: decent traction, some dirt, but no pig skin (laterite).
Up/downhills: mostly flat, only one uphill/downhill section on paved.
Shaded: 50% shaded.


----------------------------------------------------
03-2of5-Hang-Dong-Mae-Khanin-river-loop.gpx
----------------------------------------------------
Difficulty: 2/5.
Notes: if wet, beware of the slippery downhill section from km 3 to km 5.8 (pig skin, laterite). A beautiful Hmong valley with farms at km 14.4. Then 40+ easy-to-medium river crossings starting at km 21.

Terrain: 50% paved (tarmac, nice and twisty), 50% double (dirt, rivers).
Length: 43km (5 hours).
Wet conditions: potentially problematic. 3km of a very slippery downhill section with pig skin (laterite). And during the rainy season, the rivers might be uncrossable (depending on the water level).
Up/downhills: mostly flat, with a couple of gentle up/downhills.
Shaded: 40% shaded.

Legal: river section (from km 20 to km 30) is closed during smoke season, so you might be stopped by rangers.


----------------------------------------------------
04-2of5-Pa-Sang-Lamphun.gpx
----------------------------------------------------

Difficulty: 2/5.
Notes: Easy-ish and fun track. Mostly fast-flowing, gravel single track. There is one short, more technical section from km 39 to km 41 with slower, rocky single track.

Terrain: 30% double (easy dirt), 70% single (easy-medium gravel).
Length: 64km (5-6 hours).
Wet conditions: good traction, mostly gravel or earth, no pig skin (laterite).
Up/downhills: mostly flat, with a few small up/downhills.
Shaded: 50% shaded.


----------------------------------------------------
05-2of5-Red-Sands.gpx
----------------------------------------------------
Difficulty: 2/5.
Notes: Starts on road, passing through a gated community (ok to cross the gate). At km 17, stop at the watch tower, which you can climb (but be careful as it shakes a lot - not more than 2 people at a time). At km 47, there is a nice lake, a good spot for a break. Avoid detours to the sticky waterfall - too touristy. At km 72, you will pass the red sands playground - stop there to have fun jumping the small hills. The track ends on a nice viewpoint.

Length: 53km (6-7 hours).
Terrain: 10% paved (concrete), 60% double (easy dirt), 30% single (easy gravel).
Wet conditions: potentially problematic, some pig skin (laterite).
Up/downhills: mostly flat.
Shaded: 90% shaded.


----------------------------------------------------
06-2of5-Mae-Rim-West-river-and-mountain-temple.gpx
----------------------------------------------------
Difficulty: 2/5.
Notes: The route starts and finishes on a fun, all-season double track with 10+ stream crossings. The middle section takes you to the beautiful Wat Phra Putthabat Si Roi temple complex on easy double track and concrete roads. The return follows a wide, winding asphalt road, then a proper double track and a more challenging single track descent, before repeating the river track back to the start.

Terrain: 45% double track (easy to medium dirt), 45% paved, 5% single track (medium)
Length: 53 km (5-6 hours)
Wet conditions: Medium, laterite territory. The river section is fairly flat and fun when wet, but the single track and the long double track to the temple get slippery.
Climbs/descents: The first half is flat; the rest has some steep sections but mostly gentle gradients.
Shade: 70% shaded.


----------------------------------------------------
07-2of5-Chiang-Mai-to-Pai.gpx
----------------------------------------------------
Difficulty: 2/5.
Category: Adventure
Notes: This is a much more fun (but much longer) way to go to Pai by taking small village roads (mostly concrete with some offroad). The track passes through several hill-tribe villages and has beautiful vistas. But there is one (short-ish) challenging section (single up/down hill with deep ruts), were you might need assistance. The track will take you a full day of riding even at a good pace, so start early.

Terrain: 5% single (intermediate uphill), 25% double (easy-medium dirt), 40% concrete, 30% tarmac.
Length: 135km (9 hours).
Wet conditions: problematic because the short technical section can get slippery and difficult. Also, towards the end there is a river crossing, which might be difficult if the river swells with rain. Don't ride this track alone.
Up/downhills: one intermediate technical up/downhill section with ruts.
Shaded: 20% shaded.
