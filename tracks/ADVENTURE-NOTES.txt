These routes are long days on small village roads:
* Road-legal bike in good shape: adventure or dual-sport.
* Tyres that grip on concrete; watch for gravel, sand and wet leaves on concrete corners.
* Full gear: helmet, gloves, boots, jacket with armour.
* Supplies: full tank (fuel stops can be far apart), water, snacks.
Please don't ride alone!
Start early: plan a full day and don't ride after dark.
